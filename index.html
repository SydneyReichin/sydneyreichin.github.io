Featured Research

Recommendations for Assessing Vulnerabilities in Soft Targets and Crowded Places

Our latest publication offers a comprehensive review of tools used to assess vulnerabilities in soft targets and crowded places (ST-CPs), such as malls, stadiums, and places of worship. Drawing on expert interviews and current practices, we provide practical recommendations to improve assessments and guide future research in this critical area.
